Some of our favourite projects begin with a return visit. Study Milestone, a study-abroad consultancy in
Sylhet, first worked with us on a bold deep-blue office with a zone for each country. Now they have asked us to
design a second space.

## The new office

The new design is modern, functional and built for what comes next. It uses sculpted light and green walls to
give the space a calmer, more open feel than a typical consultancy office, while keeping everything students
and advisers need within easy reach.

## Where it stands

The second office is currently in the design stage. You can see the concept images in the project page, listed
as "Design" until work begins on site.

We are glad to keep building this relationship, one office at a time.

Repeat work is the best feedback we can receive. It tells us the first project did what it was meant to do,
and that the client wants to carry on with the same team.
