Study Milestone is the fastest project we have ever completed: 15 days from start to finish.

## The brief

Monzil Ahmed's study-abroad consultancy needed a confident office at Ananda Tower on Jail Road, Sylhet, covering
1,800 sq ft. We chose a deep blue as the strong, memorable base colour and organised the space into zones, one
for each country students can apply to.

## How it came together in 15 days

A tight schedule leaves no room for rework. We settled the design, the zones and the finishes before work began
on site, so the team could build without stopping to redraw.

## The result

A bold office that is easy to find your way around, with photography by Rmsi Prottoy. It has since led to a
second project with the same client, which you can read about in this journal.

Speed was never the aim on its own. The aim was an office that looks sharp on the first day and keeps working
for the people inside it.
