Designing for other people is our everyday work. Designing our own studio was something different, and it
felt like a dream coming true.

## What we built

The centrepiece is a sculpted brick feature wall carrying the Chawni mark. Around it we used warm lighting to
make the space feel like a home for the team, and a glass meeting room so that conversations with clients stay
open and bright.

## Why it matters

A studio is where ideas turn into drawings. Having our own place, built the way we believe buildings should be
built, lets clients see our approach rather than just hear about it.

You are always welcome to visit. Get in touch through the contact page and we will arrange a time.

It is also where our team of architects, civil engineers and designers works every day, so the studio doubles
as a working example of the care we put into our client projects.
