Windsor Hotel in Sylhet is finished.

We designed the whole hotel interior and carried it through to completion on site. That covers the lobby and
reception, the restaurants, the guest rooms and the corridors that connect them.

## Why it matters to us

A hotel interior is many small spaces that must feel like one place. Doing both the design and the execution
meant the drawings could be built exactly as drawn, and anything that needed adjusting on site was decided by
the same team that drew it.

## See it

The finished hotel is photographed in full in the project gallery, with photography by Rmsi Prottoy. Take a
look at the lobby, the restaurants and the rooms, and tell us what you think.

Thank you to everyone at Windsor Hotel for trusting us with the whole building.

If you are planning a hotel, a restaurant or any large interior, we would be glad to talk through what is
possible.
