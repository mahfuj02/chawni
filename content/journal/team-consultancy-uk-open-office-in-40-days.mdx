In 2021, in the middle of the second Covid wave, we delivered an office for Team Consultancy UK in just 40 days.

## The space

The office is 1,350 sq ft in Al-Madani Tower, Mirboxtula, Sylhet. We wanted it open and
full of light, so we built it from steel, steel mesh, glass and board. Those materials let daylight travel
across the floor and keep the structure honest and visible.

## The pace

A short programme in a difficult period called for simple decisions and close coordination. Choosing a small
set of materials that could be sourced and fitted quickly kept the work moving, and keeping the work simple helped it
stay on schedule.

The result is a calm, bright workplace for a student consultancy, and a reminder that good design does not have
to be slow.

If you are planning an office on a tight timeline, talk to us early. The earlier the materials and the layout
are fixed, the faster the building goes.
