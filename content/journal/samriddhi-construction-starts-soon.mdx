Samriddhi, a family residence for Subal Dhar at Nabinagar, Balaganj, is getting ready for construction.

## What we have designed

The house combines brick and timber façades with a landscaped roof terrace. Inside, every room has been fully
designed, so the interiors and the architecture arrive as one idea rather than two.

## What comes next

Before the first foundation is laid, we will finalise the details with the family and prepare the site. We will
share progress here as the building rises.

You can explore Samriddhi in 3D on its project page: turn the model, pull the floors apart, and open the
photographs from the points marked on the building.

## Why we are excited

Samriddhi is a house where the architecture, the structure and the interiors were thought about together from
the start. A brick and timber façade, deep cantilevered balconies and a landscaped roof terrace give the
family private outdoor space on several levels, and seeing it built will tell us how well those ideas hold up
on a real site.
