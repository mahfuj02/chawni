Placeholder article. Rain is not a problem to hide from; it is a condition to design with.

## 1. Deep eaves

A generous overhang protects walls and windows and gives shaded outdoor space all year.

## 2. A considered plinth

Raise the ground floor above the highest known water level and detail the entrance so water has nowhere to enter.

## 3. Drainage you can see

Visible channels and downpipes are easier to maintain than hidden ones.

## 4. Materials that dry

Choose finishes that tolerate damp and dry quickly.

## 5. Ventilation

Cross-ventilation keeps humidity down and prevents mould.
